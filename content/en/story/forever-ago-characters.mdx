```
The Anatomy of a Forever Ago Conversation:
```

## How Player Choices Shape Character Interactions

While the game follows a curated, linear road trip route, player agency plays a vital role in shaping how relationships unfold. The depth of connection Alfred builds with the people he meets is determined by your attentiveness and conversational willingness.

- **Active Listening vs. Guarded Distance:** Players can choose whether Alfred shares details about his past or keeps interactions focused on the immediate environment.

- **Scrapbook Curation:** The way you arrange snapshots and notes reflects Alfred's mental state, emphasizing either his past regrets or his new experiences.

These nuances ensure that while every player follows the same geographic highway, Alfred's emotional landscape feels unique to your choices.
