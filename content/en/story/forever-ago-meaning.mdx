One of the most effective storytelling devices in modern reflective narratives is the use of tangible artifacts. In *Forever Ago*, this mechanic takes the form of an instant camera. Players document Franz's journey by framing landscapes, forgotten objects, and fleeting interactions.

The **forever ago meaning** expands when considering how artifacts fight against the erosion of time. A photograph freezes a fraction of a second, preserving light that bounced off a subject long since changed or gone. 

### Why Material Keepsakes Matter in Storytelling

* **Sensory Triggers:** Holding an old physical print or a weathered souvenir brings forgotten sensory details instantly rushing back into the present.
* **Objective Evidence:** When our minds doubt the reality of happier eras, physical mementos serve as proof that those joyful seasons actually existed.
* **Creative Curation:** Choosing what to photograph allows a protagonist—and the audience—to curate what survives into the future.
* **Mortality Confrontation:** An artifact outlives its maker, serving as a reminder of our temporary place in the natural world.

Through framing scenes and organizing his scrapbook, Franz transforms vague memories into deliberate milestones. Instead of allowing his past to dissolve into an unreachable mist, he gives form to his experiences, turning his grief into an archive of enduring love.
