| Hardware Mode | Expected Resolution | Target Frame Rate | Critical Optimization Focus Areas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Handheld (Standard / OLED)** | 720p Dynamic | 30 FPS (Locked) | Foliage culling, downscaled shadow maps, UI font scaling |

Three primary technical pillars determine whether an intimate road trip game thrives on Nintendo's platform:

- **Typography and UI Readability:** Alfred's scrapbook, vintage postcards, and road maps contain small handwritten notes. Ensuring text scales cleanly on a seven-inch screen without pixel distortion is paramount for handheld accessibility.
- **Shader Pipeline Efficiency:** Sunlight diffusing through morning fog creates unforgettable emotional beats in *Forever Ago*. Replacing heavy volumetric fog with stylized, optimized particle meshes saves vital memory bandwidth.
- **Battery Consumption:** Thoughtful frame limiting and resource throttling prevent the console's cooling fans from drowning out delicate acoustic guitar soundtracks and quiet bird calls.
