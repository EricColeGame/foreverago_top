## Frequently Asked Questions About Forever Ago Missions

### Do forever ago missions have strict time limits or fail states?
No. *Forever Ago* is designed as an introspective, narrative-first adventure without game-over screens, death states, or ticking timers. You can explore each overlook, adjust your camera settings, and wander the forest paths at whatever pace feels comfortable. Time only advances when you trigger major travel scenes or sleep at camp.

### Can you miss optional forever ago missions during a playthrough?
Yes, certain scenic turnouts and environmental photo prompts can be bypassed if you drive directly down the main highway without stopping at scenic overlooks. However, the game clearly flags major junctions, allowing thorough players to pull over and explore side trails before moving on to the next chapter.

### How do I know if a photograph fulfilled a specific journal objective?
When you capture a frame that matches an active journal prompt, Alfred will typically offer a warm spoken reaction, or a subtle journal notification icon will appear in the corner of your screen. Upon returning to your van's desk, you can paste the new print directly over the corresponding pencil sketch in your album.

### Does the dog have its own dedicated set of mission interactions?
While the dog does not operate under a separate combat or obedience skill tree, caring for your companion is woven into nearly every chapter. Daily camp duties include feeding, petting, and playing with your dog, and several unique photo prompts center specifically on capturing your pet interacting with local wildlife and camp amenities.
