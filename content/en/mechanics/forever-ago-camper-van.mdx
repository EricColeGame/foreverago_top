## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you freely steer the camper van like an open-world driving simulator?
No. The driving segments are crafted to support the narrative rather than function as an open-world driving simulator. While players guide the vehicle along designated routes and control speed, the mechanics focus on mood, vistas, and scenic pacing rather than complex vehicular physics or off-road navigation.

### What is the main purpose of the forever ago camper van interior?
The interior of the **forever ago camper van** functions as Karl’s emotional and physical home base. Inside the cabin, players interact with mementos, develop and organize photographs, update travel journals, brew hot drinks, care for Karl's dog, and trigger reflective narrative monologues between exploration scenes.

### Can players upgrade or customize the camper van's engine and body?
The van is not customizable in the style of performance racing games. Instead, the vehicle is an established vintage classic that carries historical significance for Karl. Customization occurs organically through how players decorate the pinboard, position personal snapshots, and arrange journal pages throughout their travel.

### Does Karl's dog have dedicated interactive mechanics inside the van?
Yes, Karl's dog is an integral passenger throughout the journey. Players can pet the dog, offer treats, watch him rest in his dedicated passenger footwell bed, and call him over before sealing the cabin doors for highway travel.
