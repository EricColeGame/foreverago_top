## Frequently Asked Questions About Forever Ago Driving

### Is forever ago driving difficult for players unused to vehicle simulators?
No, the driving mechanics are designed to be approachable and stress-free. The vehicle handles with forgiving physics, smooth braking, and intuitive steering, ensuring players can focus on enjoying the scenic storytelling without worrying about vehicle damage or complex gear shifting.

### Can you crash or fail a mission during travel segments?
The game does not penalize players with catastrophic collisions, fail states, or vehicle repair costs. If you run off the shoulder or brush against a guardrail, the camper van simply slows down, allowing you to steer back onto the pavement comfortably.

### How does the camera system interact with the vehicle?
While driving, you can enjoy cinematic exterior perspectives as well as an intimate cabin view behind the steering wheel. When you pull over at overlooks, you can draw Franz's handheld camera to take framed photographs for your travel journal.

### Does the van feature interactive interior mechanics?
Yes. When parked at rest stops or evening campsites, players can freely explore the interior living space of the camper van. You can brew warm drinks, inspect personal artifacts, review your photo collection, pet Rex, and write journal entries detailing Franz's travels.
