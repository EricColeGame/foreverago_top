## Core Gameplay Mechanics to Expect at Launch

What will players actually do once the **forever ago release date** arrives? The gameplay loop in *Forever Ago* is built around observant exploration, tactile interactions, and mindful observation of the natural world.

### Mindful Photography and Journaling
Photography is far more than a decorative mini-game in *Forever Ago*. Alfred's vintage camera serves as his primary tool for processing memories and interacting with his surroundings. 

- **Framing Memories:** Players compose snapshots of landmarks, wildlife, and historical markers to uncover Alfred's thoughts.
- **Physical Photo Keepsakes:** Developing prints and organizing them in Alfred's travel scrapbook creates a permanent narrative record of your specific road trip.
- **Environmental Storytelling:** Taking photos of specific objects triggers inner monologues that explain Alfred's past and relationship with family.

### Interactive Van Life and Travel Routines
The camper van acts as your mobile sanctuary and interactive hub throughout the story. Between scenic stops, players can brew coffee, check maps, organize gear, and attend to Alfred's companion dog.
