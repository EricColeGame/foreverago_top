### Chapter 1: Whispering Pines & The Coastal Turnout

As Franz reaches the Pacific coast, the dense timber gives way to stunning ocean vistas. This section emphasizes environmental storytelling and introduces your first multi-step scenic photo challenges.

* **Explore the Rest Stop:** Park the van in the designated gravel turnout. Walk down the wooden footpath toward the weathered observation deck.
* **Restore the Coin-Operated Binoculars:** The observation viewer requires a token. Inspect the nearby picnic table and look beneath the fallen tourist brochure to locate the brass coin.
* **Photograph the Sea Stacks:** Insert the coin, gaze out toward the jagged offshore rock formations, and align Franz’s camera to capture the nesting seabirds.

### Chapter 2: The Washed-Out River Crossing

After heavy rainfall, Franz finds his path blocked by a swollen creek and a disabled timber bridge. This chapter challenges you with mechanical problem-solving and navigation.

1. **Locate the Ranger Shed:** Follow the muddy tractor path left of the bridge until you reach the locked timber shed.
2. **Find the Winch Cable:** Check the tool rack behind the generator shack to grab the heavy steel cable and D-shackle.
3. **Clear the Fallen Log:** Hook the cable around the cedar log jamming the bridge support gear, attach the winch to the front bumper of Franz’s van, and reverse slowly to clear the obstruction.
4. **Lower the Ramp:** Climb the control tower, insert the manual crank handle located inside the toolbox, and rotate the gear mechanism clockwise until the bridge locks into place.

### Chapter 3: High Ridge Vista and Solitary Camp

Climbing into the highlands presents chilly weather and quiet evening exploration. This chapter focuses heavily on character development, Franz's memories, and night-sky photography.

* **Set Up Evening Camp:** Park near the fire ring at High Ridge. Set out the folding canvas chair and light the fire pit using cedar kindling found under the lean-to.
* **The Cassette Player:** Dig into Franz’s travel trunk to find an old mixtape. Insert it into the van’s stereo to unlock a poignant flashback sequence.
* **Long Exposure Stargazing:** Mount the camera onto the wooden tripod overlooking the valley. Set the exposure dial to nighttime mode to capture the starry alpine sky.

### Chapter 4: The Abandoned Lighthouse & Final Lookout

The final major stretch brings Franz to an isolated maritime point dominated by a decommissioned lighthouse. This climactic area weaves together all exploration and puzzle mechanics.

1. **Navigate the Coastal Cliffs:** Walk along the windy footpaths, watching for scenic photo opportunities of the crashing surf.
2. **Restore Power to the Keeper's House:** Reconnect the severed junction box wires behind the storage shed (Blue to Terminal 1, Brown to Terminal 3).
3. **Access the Lantern Room:** Retrieve the brass tower key hidden inside the clockwork desk drawer on the second floor.
4. **The Final Vantage:** Step onto the exterior balcony of the lighthouse, frame the horizon as the sun breaks through the coastal fog, and take the definitive photograph of Franz’s journey.

### The River Mill Gear Alignment

In Chapter 2, operating the sluice gate at the abandoned mill requires setting four interlocking wooden cogs to the correct positions. 

* **The Problem:** The water wheel is locked, preventing the secondary generator from supplying power to the bridge winch.
* **The Solution:** 
  1. Inspect the wall plaque for the mill manufacturer's diagram.
  2. Place the largest gear (48 teeth) onto the central axle.
  3. Mount the twin medium gears (24 teeth each) on the left vertical track.
  4. Slide the small drive cog (12 teeth) to the upper right coupling.
  5. Pull the iron clutch lever to engage the water stream.

### Lighthouse Electrical Junction Box

Inside the lighthouse shed during Chapter 4, restoring power requires rewiring the blown circuit breaker board.

* **The Problem:** Fuses are tripped, and several jumper wires are corroded.
* **The Solution:**
  1. Pick up the spare 15A ceramic fuse from the workbench vice.
  2. Swap out the scorched fuse in slot "B-2".
  3. Re-route the colored patch cables according to the schematic taped to the inside cover: Red connects to Input Alpha, White connects to Ground, and Yellow bridges to Output Beta.
  4. Flip the primary master knife switch on the far left.

### Principles for Perfect Composition

* **Watch the Focus Indicator:** When peering through the viewfinder, the camera lens features an analog focus ring. Turn the ring until the focal target shifts from blurry to sharp; the crosshair turns from dull gray to soft gold.
* **Golden Hour Lighting:** Certain scenes reward you for waiting until sunset or dawn. Natural backlighting creates softer contrast and unlocks unique journal annotations.
* **Rule of Thirds:** Align key subjects—such as isolated lighthouses, soaring eagles, or Franz’s vintage camper—along the outer thirds of the viewfinder grid.
* **Macro Photography:** Don't just look at distant mountains. Crouch down near tree bark, shoreline moss, and tide pools to discover miniature ecosystems Franz can document.
