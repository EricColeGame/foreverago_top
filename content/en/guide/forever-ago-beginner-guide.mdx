## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Why do I need a forever ago beginner guide before starting the game?
Consulting a **forever ago beginner guide** helps set the right expectations for pacing, camera mechanics, and journal completion. It ensures you do not accidentally skip optional narrative sequences or miss subtle cues from your canine companion.

### Can I backtrack to previous locations if I miss a scrapbook item?
*Forever Ago* is built around a linear road trip progression. While certain major campsites allow free exploration of regional trails, driving the van forward to a new region closes previous areas. Inspecting every trail thoroughly before hitting the highway is the best way to secure a complete scrapbook.

### How does the dog assist with gameplay?
Franz's dog is more than just a loyal pet; he acts as an organic contextual guide. If you lose your bearings on unmarked forest paths, observing the dog's movement will frequently point you toward trail signs, interesting wildlife, or collectible botanical samples.

### Does photography require strict technical precision?
While the game provides tools like zoom, depth of field, and framing grids, *Forever Ago* does not penalize players with harsh grading systems. As long as the designated subject is within the viewfinder and reasonably in focus, your photo will register successfully in the scrapbook.
